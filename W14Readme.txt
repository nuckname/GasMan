Controls:

A and D to move left and right
Tab to open inventory
Escape to close game
Mouse to interact with inventory

Core Gameplay Mechanic:

The core mechanic of Gasman is about the player managing their float value which determines how high they float and is based on the average of the gas canisters float value in the player’s inventory. The player must reach the exit of the level without hitting their feet or head on the floor of the level, so they must have the right float value as it being too low or high will result in the player dying. Another mechanic of the game is the doors that the player must open by placing the right weight gas canister in the drop zone area. This adds another layer to the player’s management of their float value and inventory as if by dropping one of the gas canisters their float value must remain the same as when the door is opening they may either rise or fall if it changes their float value resulting in them dying.

Supplementary Game Mechanics:

Gasman’s supplementary mechanic is the gas automatic saving and loading mechanic that will load the player to just before they have died. Inspired by similar puzzle games which often have intricate save and load mechanics, GasMan features a saving feature that will automatically save a snapshot of the level after each major (changing gas levels) that will automatically be loaded if the player were to die. This helps address one key feedback point we had during testing where it felt awful for players to die late into a level, and have to start from scratch, which would be even worse if the player did not remember how to get back to that point in the level.
Balancing
To maintain balance and a steady difficulty curve to the gameplay the levels gradually increase in complexity. Two of the main points of feedback from playtesters were that it was a bit difficult for them to understand the mechanics at first, and that the progression of difficulty for the levels was a bit off, particularly for level 3. As such, in between the last prototype and this one we have made changes to help smooth over the learning process and difficulty curve, including moving level 3 to become a later level and adding a new teaching level in its place. 


Level Design:
The first two levels are simple tutorial levels that teach the player the basic mechanics. The (new) third level introduces doors, and teaches the player to plan ahead and place canisters in the level that they can use for later. The fourth level introduces optional collectibles that the player can collect for additional challenges. The final two levels both are both challenge levels each with their own unique challenge that the player must overcome. 

The six levels in the prototype are meant to be a broad representation of the levels that would be in the full game, containing some tutorial levels, some mechanic introducing levels and some challenging levels that provides a wide sample of what the game has to offer whilst still teaching the player and building up to the more difficult levels with a good progression of difficulty.
Assets
The assets used for the game prototype include the rigged character mode; which is the player’s avatar. There are also background assets such as boxes, tables and the hatch which help establish the setting of the game. There are also the floors of the level which define the playable space and boundaries of each level. We also have interactive assets such as the gas canisters and the doors which help the player progress throughout each level. And lastly the sound assets which are used to enhance the users immersion and also give the player cues.


Plot summary
Throughout the vastness of the cosmos, when shuttles and spacecraft encounter dangerous gas leaks, you are who they call. As The Gasman, it is your job to navigate through the exotic vapours to reach the leak and plug it. Using your personal gastank, manage your buoyancy to drift between the gaseous layers and reach your objective. Just remember not to bump your head!
